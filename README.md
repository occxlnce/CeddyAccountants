# Ceddy Accountants Website

A professional accounting firm website built for Ceddy Accountants (Pty) Ltd, a SAIPA-registered accounting practice based in Boksburg, Gauteng, South Africa.

## 🏢 About the Business

**Ceddy Accountants** is a South African accounting firm founded in 2020 by Ceddy Mokhudu Chula, a Professional Accountant (SA) and registered Tax Practitioner. The firm specializes in:

- Bookkeeping & Financial Statements
- Tax Services (VAT, PAYE, Company Tax)
- Company Registration (CIPC)
- Payroll & Compliance
- Business Advisory
- Audit Support

**Location:** Boksburg, Gauteng, South Africa  
**Registration:** 2020/643822/07  
**Professional Memberships:** SAIPA, SARS Tax Practitioner, CIPC Authorised Filer

## 🌐 Website Overview

This is a modern, responsive website showcasing Ceddy Accountants' services and expertise. The site is optimized for local SEO to help clients find accounting services in Boksburg and Gauteng.

### 📋 Pages

- **Homepage** (`index.html`) - Overview of services and value proposition
- **About** (`about.html`) - Company story, mission, vision, and values
- **Services** (`services.html`) - Detailed service offerings with interactive icons
- **Tax & Compliance** (`tax-compliance.html`) - Tax services with creative accordion interface
- **Leadership** (`leadership.html`) - Founder profile and approach
- **Contact** (`contact.html`) - Contact information and consultation booking
