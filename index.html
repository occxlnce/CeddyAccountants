About the firm

A practice built on the standard we expected from our own employers.

Ceddy Accountants (Pty) Ltd is a South African accounting firm founded in 2020 in Ekurhuleni, Gauteng. We partner with individuals, startups and small-to-medium enterprises to keep their finances current and fully compliant with South African tax and regulatory requirements.

Whether you are a sole proprietor, a registered company, a non-profit organisation or a trust, our services are tailored to your stage and structure — and grounded in the standards of the South African Institute of Professional Accountants (SAIPA).

01

Many businesses register and fall out of compliance — often without realising the obligations that follow.

The cost of catching up is always higher than the cost of staying current. Penalties, interest, lost tax clearance, refused tenders, frozen banking — these are the surprises that derail otherwise healthy businesses. We close that gap with reliable bookkeeping, accurate reporting and timely tax submissions, so you can focus on the work you actually opened the business to do.
