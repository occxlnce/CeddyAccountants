Introduction

Born from a simple observation about compliance.

Ceddy Accountants (Pty) Ltd is a South African accounting firm founded in 2020 in the City of Ekurhuleni, Gauteng Province. Established by a qualified Professional Accountant, the firm partners with individuals, startups and small-to-medium enterprises to keep their finances current and fully compliant with South African tax and regulatory requirements.

We were born from a simple observation: too many businesses register and then drift out of compliance — often unaware of the obligations that follow. Our work is to close that gap with reliable bookkeeping, accurate financial reporting and timely tax submissions, so our clients can focus on building their businesses.

Why We Exist

To close the compliance gap.

Many businesses fail to stay compliant after registration — penalties accrue, tax clearance lapses, banking gets restricted, and tenders go to better-prepared competitors.

Our role is to make sure that doesn't happen to you. We keep clients fully compliant with SARS, CIPC and statutory bodies — and financially structured for the next step, whatever it is.
