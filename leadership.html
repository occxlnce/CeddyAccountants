The Practice Lead

The standard the firm runs on, set by the person who runs the firm.

Ceddy is a qualified Professional Accountant (SA) and registered Tax Practitioner under the South African Institute of Professional Accountants (SAIPA). She founded Ceddy Accountants in 2020, drawing on her in-house corporate experience to build a practice that meets the standard she expected from her own employers.

Her work spans bookkeeping, the compilation of financial statements and the full spectrum of tax submissions for both individuals and companies. As a solo director, every engagement is led by her directly.

“Many businesses register and then drift out of compliance. Our work is to close that gap — quietly, reliably, on time.”
